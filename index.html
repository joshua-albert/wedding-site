The venue

Back to school

Edward W. Bok Technical High School
Bok
Est. 1938 · Philadelphia
Name: Anna & Joshua
Class of 2027

We're getting married in an old trade school in South Philly.

Bok is a big Art Deco building that taught Philly kids a trade for 75 years. After it closed, it was turned into studios. Today more than 200 artists, makers and small businesses work inside. There's a rooftop bar on top with a view of the whole city.

a short history

  1. 1935Construction starts
  2. 1938Opens as Edward W. Bok Technical High School
  3. 1986Added to the National Register of Historic Places
  4. 2013School closes
  5. 2014Scout buys it and turns it into workspace
  6. 2027we throw a party in the gym!

Want to babysit, or know someone who does? Email us at annaandjoshuasayido@gmail.com.

The ceremony is unplugged, so please keep your phone put away. We'll have photographers and will share everything after.
